# BSM-253 - Optimization Techniques\n\n**Credits: 4**\n\n## UNIT-I
Classical Optimization Techniques: single/multi-variable optimization with and without constraints. Non-linear
programming: Fibonacci method, Golden Section method, Quadratic Programming.
## UNIT-II
Linear Programming: graphical methods, Simplex method, Big-M method, Two-Phase method, Revised Simplex
method, Karmarkar's method, duality theorems, dual simplex method, decomposition principle.
## UNIT-III
Non-Linear Programming: unconstrained techniques - random jumping, univariate, Rosenbrock's method;
indirect search - steepest descent, Cauchy-Newton, Newton's method; transportation and assignment problems.
## UNIT-IV
Geometric Programming: polynomial, unconstrained minimization, degree of difficulty, constrained minimization,
complementary geometric programming, applications, deterministic dynamic programming.
