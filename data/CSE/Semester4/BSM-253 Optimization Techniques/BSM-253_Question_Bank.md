# BSM-253 - Optimization Techniques
## Complete Unit-Wise Question Bank (Theory + Numericals, Basic → Advanced)

> **Course:** BSM-253 - Optimization Techniques  
> **Credits:** 4  
> **Coverage:** Strictly mapped to the syllabus.

## Syllabus Reference
UNIT-I  
Classical Optimization Techniques: single/multi-variable optimization with and without constraints. Non-linear programming: Fibonacci method, Golden Section method, Quadratic Programming.

UNIT-II  
Linear Programming: graphical methods, Simplex method, Big-M method, Two-Phase method, Revised Simplex method, Karmarkar's method, duality theorems, dual simplex method, decomposition principle.

UNIT-III  
Non-Linear Programming: unconstrained techniques - random jumping, univariate, Rosenbrock's method; indirect search - steepest descent, Cauchy-Newton, Newton's method; transportation and assignment problems.

UNIT-IV  
Geometric Programming: polynomial, unconstrained minimization, degree of difficulty, constrained minimization, complementary geometric programming, applications, deterministic dynamic programming.
