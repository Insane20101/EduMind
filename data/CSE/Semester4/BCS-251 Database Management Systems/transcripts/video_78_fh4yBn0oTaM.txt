The method is very simple:
```sql
Select id, salary 
From emp e1 
Where N-1 = (
    Select count(distinct salary) 
    From emp e2 
    Where e2.salary > e1.salary
);
```

What is $N$ over here? $N^{\text{th}}$ is the highest salary that you have to find. Let's say you have to find the 5th highest, then put 5 over here ($5-1 = 4$). 

`= select count(distinct salary)`
We have to find the count over here of distinct salaries from `emp e2`. We have made one more alias, `e2`, where `e2.salary > e1.salary`. 

So over here, we have written a correlated nested query. How do we recognize a correlated nested query? Where some value of the outer query is used in the inner query. See the employee table in the outer query: we have made an alias `e1`, and `e1` is used internally in this query. We call it a correlated nested query, and I have already made a video regarding correlated nested queries and its link I have given in the description box.
