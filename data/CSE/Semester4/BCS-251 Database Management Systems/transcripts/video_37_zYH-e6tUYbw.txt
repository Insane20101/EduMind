What are joins? It is clear from the name itself: when we have to join two or more tables to find any result, meaning we have to find such a result which we are not getting from a single table. 

For that, I have made one simple example here. Let's say there is an employee table. The employee table has three attributes: employee's number, employee's name, and address. Now, if you see carefully, what information can I get from the employee table? Suppose I have to find out an employee's ID whose name is Varun, or tell the employee's name whose ID is a certain value, or tell the address of an employee. Let's say I ask a simple question: "I have to find out the address of the employee whose name is Varun." For this, I only need the help of the employee table; I can find out the data directly from it. How do we write the query? There is a simple way of writing the query:
`SELECT address FROM employee WHERE E_name = 'Varun'`
Where `E_name` is equal to Varun, its address will be finally printed out. What will come in the final output? CHD, that is Chandigarh.

In this table, it will act as a foreign key, taking reference from the primary key (meaning in the employee table, employee number is the primary key, and from here it will take reference).
