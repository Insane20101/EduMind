# SCS-231 - Computer Graphics for Virtual Reality
## Complete Unit-Wise Question Bank (Theory + Numericals, Basic → Advanced)

> **Course:** SCS-231 - Computer Graphics for Virtual Reality  
> **Credits:** 3  
> **Coverage:** Strictly mapped to the syllabus.

## Syllabus Reference
- UNIT-I: Graphics system and models, geometric objects and transformations, coordinate systems/frames, affine transformations, homogeneous coordinates, vertices to fragments - clipping, rasterization, hidden-surface removal, antialiasing.
- UNIT-II: Lighting and shading: Phong reflection model, polygonal shading, per-fragment lighting, global illumination. Hierarchical modelling: symbols/instances, trees, scene graphs.
- UNIT-III: Discrete techniques: buffers, texture mapping, environment/bump mapping, compositing, sampling and aliasing. Advanced rendering: ray tracing, radiosity, volume rendering, mesh simplification, image-based rendering.
- UNIT-IV: Fractals: Sierpinski Gasket, coastline problem, Koch curves, fractal dimension, turtle graphics, Mandelbrot set. Virtual Reality Modelling Language, stereo display programming, navigation modes.

## UNIT I — Graphics Systems and Models
### Section A: Definitions & Concept Questions (Basic, 2–3 Marks)

1. **[Unit I | Topic: Graphics Systems | Type: Theory | Difficulty: Basic]**  
   Define a graphics system and explain its main components.

2. **[Unit I | Topic: Geometric Objects | Type: Theory | Difficulty: Basic]**  
   What are geometric objects in computer graphics? Provide examples.

3. **[Unit I | Topic: Affine Transformations | Type: Theory | Difficulty: Basic]**  
   Explain the concept of affine transformations and give examples.

### Section B: Computational & Applied Problems (Intermediate, 7 Marks)

1. **[Unit I | Topic: Clipping | Type: Numerical | Difficulty: Intermediate]**  
   Given a line segment defined by endpoints A(1, 2) and B(4, 5), apply the Cohen-Sutherland clipping algorithm within a clipping window defined by coordinates (0, 0) and (3, 3).

2. **[Unit I | Topic: Rasterization | Type: Numerical | Difficulty: Intermediate]**  
   Describe the rasterization process for a triangle with vertices at (1, 1), (5, 1), and (3, 4). Calculate the pixel coverage.

### Section C: Advanced Theory & Numericals

1. **[Unit I | Topic: Hidden-Surface Removal | Type: Theory | Difficulty: Advanced]**  
   Discuss various techniques for hidden-surface removal and their advantages and disadvantages.

2. **[Unit I | Topic: Antialiasing | Type: Theory | Difficulty: Advanced]**  
   Explain the concept of antialiasing in computer graphics and describe techniques to minimize aliasing artifacts.
