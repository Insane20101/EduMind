# SCS-231 - Computer Graphics for Virtual Reality (Dept. Minor)\n\n**Credits: 3**\n\n## UNIT-I
Graphics system and models, geometric objects and transformations, coordinate systems/frames, affine
transformations, homogeneous coordinates, vertices to fragments - clipping, rasterization, hidden-surface
removal, antialiasing.
## UNIT-II
Lighting and shading: Phong reflection model, polygonal shading, per-fragment lighting, global illumination.
Hierarchical modelling: symbols/instances, trees, scene graphs.
## UNIT-III
Discrete techniques: buffers, texture mapping, environment/bump mapping, compositing, sampling and aliasing.
Advanced rendering: ray tracing, radiosity, volume rendering, mesh simplification, image-based rendering.
## UNIT-IV
Fractals: Sierpinski Gasket, coastline problem, Koch curves, fractal dimension, turtle graphics, Mandelbrot set.
Virtual Reality Modelling Language, stereo display programming, navigation modes.
