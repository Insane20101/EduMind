# SCS-221 - Introduction to Security of Cyber-Physical Systems
## Complete Unit-Wise Question Bank (Theory + Numericals, Basic → Advanced)

> **Course:** SCS-221 - Introduction to Security of Cyber-Physical Systems  
> **Credits:** 3  
> **Coverage:** Strictly mapped to the syllabus.

## Syllabus Reference
- UNIT-I: Overview of Security and Privacy in Information Systems, applied cryptography and intrusion detection, one-way hash functions and integrity, encryption algorithms, digital signatures and authentication, information theory.
- UNIT-II: Internet of Things Security: case studies - Smart Home, Smart Grid Network, Modern Vehicle, Wearable Computing & BYOD, Mobile Healthcare.
- UNIT-III: Software-Defined Networks: introduction, security, privacy leakages, case studies on attacking SDN.
- UNIT-IV: Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS): platform components, implementation issues, intelligent CPS, secure deployment of CPS.
