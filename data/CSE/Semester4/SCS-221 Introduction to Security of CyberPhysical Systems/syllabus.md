# SCS-221 - Introduction to Security of Cyber-Physical Systems (Dept. Minor)\n\n**Credits: 3**\n\n## UNIT-I
Overview of Security and Privacy in Information Systems, applied cryptography and intrusion detection, one-way
hash functions and integrity, encryption algorithms, digital signatures and authentication, information theory.
## UNIT-II
Internet of Things Security: case studies - Smart Home, Smart Grid Network, Modern Vehicle, Wearable
Computing & BYOD, Mobile Healthcare.
## UNIT-III
Software-Defined Networks: introduction, security, privacy leakages, case studies on attacking SDN.
## UNIT-IV
Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS): platform components, implementation issues, intelligent CPS, secure
deployment of CPS.
