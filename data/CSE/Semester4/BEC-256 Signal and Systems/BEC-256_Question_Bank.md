# BEC-256 - Signals and Systems
## Complete Unit-Wise Question Bank (Theory + Numericals, Basic → Advanced)

> **Course:** BEC-256 - Signals and Systems  
> **Credits:** 4  
> **Coverage:** Strictly mapped to the syllabus.

## Syllabus Reference
**UNIT-I**  
Signals: definitions, types and representations, common signals, operations on signals. Systems: classification, linearity, time-invariance, causality, impulse response, characterization of LTI systems, convolution, stability, poles and zeros.

**UNIT-II**  
Fourier Series and Fourier Transforms: FS representation and properties, FT existence/properties/magnitude-phase spectra, Parseval's theorem, inverse FT, Discrete Time Fourier Transform (DTFT).

**UNIT-III**  
Laplace Transform and Z-transform: one-sided LT, theorems and properties, inverse LT, solving differential equations, bilateral LT, ROC; one/bilateral Z-transforms, properties, solving difference equations, s-to-Z plane mapping.

**UNIT-IV**  
Time and frequency domain analysis: convolution integral, correlations, energy/power spectral density, first and second order system analysis, system functions and block diagram representations, system bandwidth and rise time.
