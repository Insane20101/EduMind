# BEC-256 - Signals and Systems\n\n**Credits: 4**\n\n## UNIT-I
Signals: definitions, types and representations, common signals, operations on signals. Systems: classification,
linearity, time-invariance, causality, impulse response, characterization of LTI systems, convolution, stability,
poles and zeros.
## UNIT-II
Fourier Series and Fourier Transforms: FS representation and properties, FT
existence/properties/magnitude-phase spectra, Parseval's theorem, inverse FT, Discrete Time Fourier
Transform (DTFT).
## UNIT-III
Laplace Transform and Z-transform: one-sided LT, theorems and properties, inverse LT, solving differential
equations, bilateral LT, ROC; one/bilateral Z-transforms, properties, solving difference equations, s-to-Z plane
mapping.
## UNIT-IV
Time and frequency domain analysis: convolution integral, correlations, energy/power spectral density, first and
second order system analysis, system functions and block diagram representations, system bandwidth and rise
time.
