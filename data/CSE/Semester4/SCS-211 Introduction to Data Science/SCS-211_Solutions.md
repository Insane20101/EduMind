### Section A: Definitions & Concept Questions

**Q1. [Unit I | Topic: Introduction to Data Science | Type: Theory | Difficulty: Basic]**  
**Question:** What is Data Science and why is it important in today's world?  

*Concept from scratch:* Data Science is an interdisciplinary field that uses scientific methods, processes, algorithms, and systems to extract knowledge and insights from structured and unstructured data. It combines techniques from statistics, computer science, and domain knowledge to analyze and interpret complex data, enabling informed decision-making. Its importance in today’s world stems from the exponential growth of data and the need for businesses and organizations to leverage this data to gain competitive advantages, improve operational efficiency, and enhance customer experiences.  

*Step 1 — Define Data Science:* Data Science involves data collection, cleaning, analysis, and interpretation.  

*Step 2 — Importance:* It helps organizations make data-driven decisions, predict trends, and personalize services.  

*Result:* Data Science is crucial as it enables organizations to harness large volumes of data for strategic decision-making, thereby driving innovation and improving service delivery.

### Section B: Computational & Applied Problems

**Q1. [Unit III | Topic: Feature Selection Algorithms | Type: Theory | Difficulty: Intermediate]**  
**Question:** Describe and compare two feature selection algorithms used in Data Science.  

*Concept from scratch:* Feature selection algorithms are techniques used to identify the most relevant features for model building. Two common algorithms are Recursive Feature Elimination (RFE) and LASSO (Least Absolute Shrinkage and Selection Operator).  

*Step 1 — Explain RFE:* RFE works by recursively removing the least important features based on the model's performance until the desired number of features is reached. It uses cross-validation to avoid overfitting.  

*Step 2 — Explain LASSO:* LASSO is a regression analysis method that includes a penalty term for the absolute value of the coefficients, effectively shrinking some coefficients to zero, thus performing feature selection.  

*Step 3 — Compare Algorithms:* RFE is model-agnostic and can be used with any estimator, while LASSO is specific to linear models. RFE is computationally intensive, whereas LASSO is faster due to its mathematical formulation.  

*Result:* RFE and LASSO are both effective for feature selection, with RFE being model-agnostic and LASSO being a regularization technique that simultaneously selects features.

### Section A: Definitions & Concept Questions

**Q1. [Unit IV | Topic: Data Visualization Principles | Type: Theory | Difficulty: Basic]**  
**Question:** What are the basic principles of data visualization?  

*Concept from scratch:* Data visualization principles encompass guidelines for creating effective visual representations of data that facilitate understanding and insight.  

*Step 1 — Clarity:* Visualizations should clearly convey the intended message without ambiguity.  

*Step 2 — Accuracy:* Represent data accurately to avoid misleading interpretations.  

*Step 3 — Simplicity:* Keep visualizations simple, avoiding unnecessary complexity that can confuse viewers.
