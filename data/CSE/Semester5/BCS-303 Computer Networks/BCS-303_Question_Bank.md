# BCS-303 - Computer Networks
## Complete Unit-Wise Question Bank (Theory + Numericals, Basic → Advanced)

> **Course:** BCS-303 - Computer Networks  
> **Credits:** 4  
> **Coverage:** Strictly mapped to the syllabus.

## Syllabus Reference
- UNIT-I: Data Communication Concepts, networks and layered architecture, protocols and standards, OSI and TCP/IP reference models, transmission media, network topology, multiplexing, circuit/packet switching, LAN and interconnection devices.
- UNIT-II: Medium Access Sub Layer: channel allocation, LAN protocols, ALOHA protocols, CSMA/CSMA-CD, collision-free protocols, IEEE standards, Ethernet, FDDI. Data Link Layer: design issues, error detection/correction, sliding window protocols, HDLC.
- UNIT-III: Network Layer: packet switched networks - IP, ARP, RARP, DHCP, ICMP, queuing discipline, routing algorithms, congestion control, internetworking, TCP/IP protocol, IPv4 and IPv6.
- UNIT-IV: Transport Layer: design issues, connection management, UDP, TCP, adaptive retransmission, congestion control/avoidance, QoS. Application Layer: DNS, Email, FTP, HTTP, introduction to cryptography and network security.

## UNIT I — Data Communication Concepts
### Section A: Definitions & Concept Questions (Basic, 2–3 Marks)

1. **[Unit I | Topic: Data Communication Concepts | Type: Theory | Difficulty: Basic]**  
   Define data communication and explain its significance in computer networks.

2. **[Unit I | Topic: OSI Model | Type: Theory | Difficulty: Basic]**  
   What are the seven layers of the OSI model? Briefly describe the function of each layer.

3. **[Unit I | Topic: TCP/IP Model | Type: Theory | Difficulty: Basic]**  
   Describe the TCP/IP model and how it differs from the OSI model.

### Section B: Computational & Applied Problems (Intermediate, 7 Marks)

1. **[Unit I | Topic: Network Topology | Type: Numerical | Difficulty: Intermediate]**  
   Given a star topology with 5 devices, calculate the number of connections required to connect all devices to the central hub.

2. **[Unit I | Topic: Multiplexing | Type: Numerical | Difficulty: Intermediate]**  
   If a time-division multiplexing system has 8 channels, each requiring 125 Kbps, what is the minimum bandwidth required?

### Section C: Advanced Theory & Numericals

1. **[Unit I | Topic: Circuit vs Packet Switching | Type: Theory | Difficulty: Advanced]**  
   Compare and contrast circuit switching and packet switching in terms of efficiency and usability.

2. **[Unit I | Topic: Interconnection Devices | Type: Theory | Difficulty: Advanced]**  
   Discuss the role of routers and switches in a network. How do they differ in functionality?
