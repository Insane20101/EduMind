To maine computer network ke saare important topics ki video already cover kar di hai, playlist mein aapko mil jayegi. Lekin maine socha ki kyu na basics ko bhi complete kiya jaaye, toh isliye sabse pehle yahan par aayi hai Personal Area Network, that is PAN; Local Area Network, that is LAN; Campus Area Network, that is CAN; Metropolitan Area Network, that is MAN; aur Wide Area Network, that is WAN. 

Sabse pehle inki jo full form hai, wo aapko pata honi chahiye. Toh isme jo beech mein comparison hai, wo saare ke saare distance ke upar hai, matlab hamare paas Personal Area Network, uske baad Local Area Network, uske baad hamare paas aa gaya Campus Area Network, uske baad hamare paas aa gaya Metropolitan Area Network, aur finally hamare paas kya aa gaya Wide Area Network. Pehle toh inke beech mein aapko ye pata hona chahiye ki actual mein inke beech mein jo major difference hai, wo hai distance, that is range aap bol sakte ho. 

Aur yahan par baat karein toh sabse pehle PAN, yani ki Personal Area Network. Isme agar range ki baat karein toh kitni hai? 0 to 10 meters, matlab yahan par jo maximum range hai, wo iski meters tak hai, 10 meters tak hai, aur within a room, aap isko alag room ke andar-andar jo hai, wo use kar rahe hain. Isme technology aapne suna hoga Bluetooth. Bluetooth ke through aap apne phone ko apne laptop se connect kar rahe ho, ya apne friends ko kuch data transfer kar rahe ho, toh wo aapka PAN ho gaya. Iske alawa, Infrared technology aap use kar sakte ho jo ki TV remote mein hoti hai, aur Wi-Fi, hotspot, ye sab bhi iske andar hi aate hain, but at least aapko name jo hai, wo pata hone chahiye. 

Doosra agar baat karein yahan par transmission speed ki, toh isme jo distance hai, wo bahut kam hai, jo distance hai wo room ke barabar hai, toh isliye meri jo transmission speed hai, wo kya hogi? High hogi. Ownership ki baat karein toh, ownership matlab isko kaun kar raha hai? Toh bhai maine apne system mein, matlab maine apne mobile se Bluetooth on kiya aur maine apne friends ko ya apne device ko connect kiya, toh matlab agar mera khud ka hai, toh koi third party thodi na hai. Toh obviously ownership isme kya hogi? Private. 

Maintenance ki baat karein toh yaar isme kya maintain karna hai? Aapko koi extra hardware, koi software thodi na install karne ki zaroorat hai, inbuilt toh hota hai. Toh isme aap jo keh sakte ho, transmission aur jo maintenance hai, wo very easy hai. Cost ki baat karein toh obviously very low. Cost bhi, jo hai, wo distance ke upar base hai, distance jitna bhi kam hoga, error ke chances utne kam honge aur cost bhi aapki utni kam hogi, kyunki aapko extra routers, switches, ye sab extra hardware lagane ki zaroorat nahi hai. Toh obviously, very low yahan par. Toh ye saare points hai, wo PAN mein yaad rakhna. 

Iske baad aate hain Local Area Network, yani ki LAN. Jahaan par range jo hai, wo badh jaati hai. Toh yahan par aap 1 kilometer to 10 kilometers tak ki range le sakte ho. Jiske andar aap kisi school ki baat kar lo, kisi university ki baat kar lo, ya fir apne ghar ke andar jo Wi-Fi router hota hai, wo hum log use karte hain, toh wo ek tarah se Local Area Network hai. Yahan par agar baat karein technologies ki, toh Ethernet use karte hain. Pehle Coaxial cable use karte the, jaise BNC connectors, jo aapne PDS mein padhe honge, toh wahan par hum Coaxial cable use karte the, lekin ab hum Twisted-pair cable ya Wi-Fi ke upar kisi bhi tarah ki technology use kar sakte hain. 

Transmission speed ki baat karein toh obviously high hogi, kyunki distance iske andar bhi limited hai, 1 to 10 kilometers. Ownership ki baat karein toh, private. Obviously, main khud bana raha hoon, mere ghar ke andar mera network ban raha hai, ya meri school ke andar mera network ban raha hai, toh obviously network kya hai? Private hai. Maintenance bhi aur kam hogi, aur cost bhi meri kam hogi, aur error rates jo hai, wo bhi bahut low iske andar honge. Isme aapko kahin na kahin devices ko, switches ko, hubs ko lagane ki zaroorat padegi, kyunki yahan par range thodi si badh gayi hai. 

Iske baad aate hain Campus Area Network, yani ki CAN. Jisko hum Corporate Area Network bhi bolte hain. Yahan par range jo hai, wo thodi aur badh jaati hai LAN se, toh 1 to 5 kilometers tak ki range ho jaati hai. Example ke taur par, kisi university ki baat kar lo, ya kisi bade industrial area ki baat kar lo, ya fir kisi badi corporate office ki baat kar lo, jaise Microsoft hai, toh Microsoft ke andar bahut saari buildings hongi, toh unhone apna network banaya hoga, wo aap bol sakte ho. Ya koi University ki baat kar lo, Stanford University ki baat kar lo, BITS ki baat kar lo, toh wahan par agar unhone apne campus ke andar banaya hoga network, toh wo ek tarah se Campus Area Network hoga. LAN se thoda sa bada, aap keh sakte ho. 

Campus Area Network ki ownership bhi, obviously, private, kyunki kisi University ya college ne apna banaya hai, toh campus ke andar hoga. Moderate iski maintenance hogi, kyunki chaar-paanch buildings, aath-das buildings aapas mein connect ho rahi hain, toh kahin na kahin aapko devices bhi zyada lagane pad rahe hain, thoda distance badh gaya hai, toh kahin na kahin aapki jo maintenance hai, uske andar thodi aapko moderate, matlab thodi zyada maintain karna padega. Rate aur cost ki baat karein toh, obviously badh jaati hai, jaise-jaise idhar ja rahe hain, distance badh raha hai, toh cheezein apne aap uske according vary ho rahi hain. 

That is Metropolitan Area Network, yani ki MAN. Yahan par agar baat karein range ki, toh 5 to 50 kilometers, jahaan par hum Metropolitan cities ki baat karte hain, Metro cities ki baat, jaise Mumbai ki baat kar lo, Kolkata ki baat kar lo, Chennai ki baat kar lo, toh wahan par aap MAN use karte hain. Ownership ki baat karein toh private bhi ho sakta hai aur public bhi ho sakta hai. Koi cable operator hai, toh wo apna MAN use kar raha hai, ya government apna banana chahti hai, toh wo bhi bana sakti hai. 

Maintenance ki baat karein toh difficult ho jaata hai, kyunki distance itna zyada hai, 5 to 50 kilometers. Toh har kuch time ke baad aapko devices lagane pad rahe hain, kuch-kuch doori par jo towers hain, ya fir aap keh sakte ho jo wires hain, unko manage karna thoda mushkil ho gaya hai, toh maintenance thodi difficult hai. Error rates moderate, transmission speed bhi moderate, ki aap wahan par kitne distance ke upar jo hai, wo use kar rahe hain. Jaise layer aate hain, jaise aapke cable operator ki speed ke upar depend karta hai. Agar baat karein technologies ki, toh yahan par technology kaunsi use ho rahi hai? FDDI, yaani ki Fiber Distributed Data Interface, aur saath hi saath ATM, yaani ki Asynchronous Transfer Mode, techniques jo hai, wo yahan par use ki jaati hain. 

Wide Area Network, yaani ki WAN, jisko hum kya bolte hain? Internet bolte hain. This is the broadest range, jo ki countries ko aapas mein kya kar rahi hai? Connect kar rahi hai. Isko www, aap bol sakte ho, Wide Area Network. Toh WWW, aap keh sakte ho, World Wide Web, wo bhi ek tarah se internet ka hi part hai. Toh internet jo hai, wo aapka Wide Area Network hai. Sabse badi range, aap isko le sakte hain. Ye kaise banate hain? Leased lines se, dial-up connections se. Leased lines kya hoti hain? Bahut saari companies, Airtel ki baat kar lo, Jio ki baat kar lo, unhone leased line de rakhi hai, aur aap kya karte ho unke through connect kar rahe ho internet se. Toh ye leased line aap yahan use karte hain. 

Agar baat karein transmission speed ki, toh yahan par transmission speed comparative low hoti hai. Aisa nahi hai ki low hai, tabhi aapko gigabits speed mil rahi hogi, lekin comparative yahan par baat karein toh kya hogi? Low hogi, kyunki distance bahut zyada hai. Ownership: Private bhi ho sakta hai, public bhi ho sakta hai, but mostly public, kyunki internet toh aapko pata hai ki public hai. Maintenance very difficult, kyunki yahan par distance badh gaya, devices badh gaye, log badh gaye, toh isliye aapke error rates ke bhi chances zyada hain, cost bhi badhegi, aur manage karna bhi difficult hoga. 

Toh actual mein internet ka jo concept hai, wo baad mein aaya hai. Internet ki jo idea hai, wo kahan se li hai? Wo telephone network se li hai. 1980s ke time par telephone network aaya tha, ya fir 1980s ke baad 1990s ke time par aapka kya hua tha? Ye internet ka concept aaya tha. Toh jab telephone network ko use karte hain, toh aapne suna hoga, PC-O, STD, ISD. Toh PC-O hota hai local calls ke liye, STD inter-state ke liye, aur ISD international ke liye. Toh aap ek tarah se LAN ko kya bol sakte ho? PC-O ka andar, aur MAN ko aap kya bol sakte ho? Ek tarah se STD, aur WAN ko aap kya bol sakte ho? Ek tarah se ISD. Toh ye aapke paas ek tarah se aa gaya, aur baaki ki cheezein thodi extend ki hui hain.
