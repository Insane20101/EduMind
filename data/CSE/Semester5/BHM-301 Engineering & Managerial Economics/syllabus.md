# BHM-301 - Engineering & Managerial Economics\n\n**Credits: 2**\n\n## UNIT-I
Introduction to Managerial Economics: economics and managerial economics, review of economic terms, law of
diminishing marginal utility, theories of profit, decision making process, applications in engineering.
## UNIT-II
Theory of Demand: law of demand, demand function, types, demand schedule/curve, shift in demand, elasticity,
theory of consumer behaviour, demand forecasting techniques.
## UNIT-III
Theory of Supply: law of supply, supply function/schedule/curve, factors affecting supply. Types of cost: fixed,
variable, average, marginal, opportunity cost, economies of scale.
## UNIT-IV
Market Structure: perfect competition, imperfect competition (monopolistic, oligopoly, monopoly), inflation -
meaning, types, causes, deflation, business cycle.
