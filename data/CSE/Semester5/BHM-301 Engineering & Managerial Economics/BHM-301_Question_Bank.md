# BHM-301 - Engineering & Managerial Economics
## Complete Unit-Wise Question Bank (Theory + Numericals, Basic → Advanced)

> **Course:** BHM-301 - Engineering & Managerial Economics  
> **Credits:** 2  
> **Coverage:** Strictly mapped to the syllabus.

## Syllabus Reference
### UNIT-I
Introduction to Managerial Economics: economics and managerial economics, review of economic terms, law of diminishing marginal utility, theories of profit, decision making process, applications in engineering.

### UNIT-II
Theory of Demand: law of demand, demand function, types, demand schedule/curve, shift in demand, elasticity, theory of consumer behaviour, demand forecasting techniques.

### UNIT-III
Theory of Supply: law of supply, supply function/schedule/curve, factors affecting supply. Types of cost: fixed, variable, average, marginal, opportunity cost, economies of scale.

### UNIT-IV
Market Structure: perfect competition, imperfect competition (monopolistic, oligopoly, monopoly), inflation - meaning, types, causes, deflation, business cycle.
