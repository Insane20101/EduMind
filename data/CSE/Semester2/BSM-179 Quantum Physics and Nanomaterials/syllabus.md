# BSM-179 - Quantum Physics and Nanomaterials\n\n**Credits: 4**\n\n## UNIT-I
Solid State Physics: Band theory of solids, conductors/insulators/semiconductors, thermal properties (lattice
vibration, specific heat - Debye and Einstein theory), magnetic properties (dia, para, ferro magnetism).
## UNIT-II
Semiconductor Devices: intrinsic/extrinsic semiconductors, p-n junction, transistors (p-n-p, n-p-n), amplifiers,
oscillators, op-amps, FET and MOSFET, digital electronics (Boolean algebra, De Morgan's laws, logic gates,
truth tables).
## UNIT-III
Quantum Mechanics: De Broglie waves and group velocity, uncertainty principle, Davisson-Germer and
Stern-Gerlach experiments, electron spin, Schrodinger equation (time dependent/independent), postulates of
quantum mechanics, particle in a box.
## UNIT-IV
Physics of Advanced Materials: temperature dependence of resistivity in superconductors, Meissner effect, Type
I and Type II superconductors, BCS theory (qualitative); Nanomaterials: introduction to nanoscience and
technology.
