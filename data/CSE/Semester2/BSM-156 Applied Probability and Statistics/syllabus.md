# BSM-156 - Applied Probability and Statistics\n\n**Credits: 4**\n\n## UNIT-I
Basic Statistics: frequency distribution, mean, median, mode, moments, moment generating function, skewness
and its types, kurtosis. Curve fitting: method of least squares, fitting straight lines and parabola of second
degree.
## UNIT-II
Applied Statistics: correlation, correlation coefficient, Spearman's rank correlation, regression, equations of
regression lines, linear and non-linear regression analysis, relation between regression and correlation.
## UNIT-III
Probability: random experiment, outcome, trial and event, exhaustive/favourable/independent events, sample
space, classical and empirical definitions, addition and multiplication theorems, conditional probability, Bayes'
theorem.
## UNIT-IV
Probability Distribution: discrete and continuous random variables, distribution functions, Binomial, Poisson and
Normal distributions and their parameters. Test of significance: sampling, large sample tests, Chi-square test for
goodness of fit.
