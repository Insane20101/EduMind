# BHM-113 - Industrial Psychology\n\n**Credits: 2**\n\n## UNIT-I
Introduction to Industrial Psychology: nature, importance and scope, Scientific management, Time and motion
study, Human relations school.
## UNIT-II
Individual in Workplace: Motivation and job satisfaction, Stress management, Organisational culture, Leadership
and group dynamics.
## UNIT-III
Work Environment, Recruitment and Selection: Engineering Psychology, fatigue and boredom, work
environment, accident and safety, job analysis, recruitment/selection, psychological tests.
## UNIT-IV
Performance Management and Training: performance appraisal - importance and methods, training and
development concepts and benefits to the organization.
