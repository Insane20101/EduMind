# BSM-144 - Environment and Ecology\n\n**Credits: 2**\n\n## UNIT-I
Basic concepts of Environmental Chemistry, atmospheric chemistry, layers of atmosphere and chemical
composition, chemistry of gaseous and particulate pollutants, Ozone and its control, Green House Effect.
## UNIT-II
Chemistry of Natural Waters, Oxidation-Reduction Chemistry, Ion Concentrations in Natural and Potable Water,
Water Pollution and Purification, Water Disinfection, Ground Water Supply and Contamination,
Wastewater/Sewage Treatment.
## UNIT-III
Toxic Heavy Metals (Mercury, Lead, Arsenic, Cadmium), Soil Pollution, Domestic and Commercial Garbage,
Solid Waste Management, Recycling, Hazardous Wastes and Disposal Methods.
## UNIT-IV
Toxic Organic Compounds, Pesticides, Insecticides, Herbicides, Dioxins, Furans, PCBs, Polynuclear Aromatic
Hydrocarbons, Chemistry of Food Additives, Dyes, Detergents and Bleaching Agents.
