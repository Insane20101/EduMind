# BCS-103 - Computer Troubleshooting & Maintenance\n\n**Credits: 2**\n\n## Course Focus
Hands-on lab course: hardware components, disassembling/assembling a PC, ports and connectors, BIOS
settings, low/high level formatting and partitioning, installing OS, printer/scanner/peripheral installation,
diagnosing common PC problems, registry maintenance, preventive maintenance, anti-virus, virtualization
basics, and Windows networking/file sharing.
