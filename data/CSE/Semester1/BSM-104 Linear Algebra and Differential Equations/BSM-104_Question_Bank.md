## How to Use This Set
- Work Section A first in every unit — these check whether definitions/statements are solid.
- Section B builds direct computational fluency (the bulk of exam marks come from here).
- Section C is exam-simulation level — attempt these under time pressure (18–20 minutes each) since they mirror the actual "attempt one of two (OR)" format.
- Repeat weak units after a first pass rather than moving linearly — Sequences/Series (Unit I) and Differential Equations (Unit IV) usually need the most repetition for fluency with tests/operator methods.
