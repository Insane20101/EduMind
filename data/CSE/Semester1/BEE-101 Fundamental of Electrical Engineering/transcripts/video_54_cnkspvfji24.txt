**Factors Affecting Speed Control**

Next, let's examine the factors that affect the speed of a DC motor. The speed of a DC motor depends on several key factors, which we will explore in detail. 

1. **Supply Voltage**: The supply voltage provided to the motor plays a significant role in determining its speed. Higher voltage typically results in higher speed, while lower voltage reduces speed.

2. **Armature Resistance**: The resistance within the armature also affects the motor's speed. A higher resistance can lead to a drop in speed due to increased losses.

3. **Field Strength**: The strength of the magnetic field generated by the field windings impacts the motor's speed. A stronger magnetic field can enhance the motor's performance and speed.

4. **Load Conditions**: The load on the motor significantly influences its speed. As the load increases, the speed may decrease unless compensated by increasing the voltage or adjusting other parameters.
