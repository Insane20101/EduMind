**Balanced System Characteristics**

In a balanced system, all line currents are equal, and all phase currents are also equal. The line voltages will also be equal to each other. This means:

- All line voltages (V_L) are equal.
- All phase voltages (V_F) are equal.
- All line currents (I_L) are equal.
- All phase currents (I_F) are equal.

This balance is essential for the stability and efficiency of the electrical system.
