This is where 'HTML, Semantic Tags' and the concept of 'HTML5 introduced arrangement of Semantic Tags' comes in.

The initial versions of HTML did not include semantic tags. Semantic tags were introduced with HTML5. The primary reason for their introduction in HTML5 was to arrange content logically. This ensures that any entity parsing the content or examining our code—be it a browser, a crawler, Google, Bing, other search engines, or even a tool generating a preview (like when you share a URL on Twitter and a preview is generated)—can properly understand the content.
