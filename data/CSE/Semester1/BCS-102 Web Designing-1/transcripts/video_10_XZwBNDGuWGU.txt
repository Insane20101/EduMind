*   **`none`**: This is the default value. It indicates that the browser should not preload the audio at all. Preload means if you want the browser to fetch the data from the beginning, then you will use the `preload` attribute; otherwise, you will not.
*   **`metadata`**: This means the browser will preload only the information about the audio file, such as its duration.
*   **`auto`**: This means the browser will preload the entire audio file. So, `preload="auto"` will download and automatically load the audio, so that whenever the user plays it, it will be instantly available.
