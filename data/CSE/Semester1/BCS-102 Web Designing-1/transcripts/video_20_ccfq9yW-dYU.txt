In mathematics, factorial means the multiplication of a number with all the smaller positive integers until 1 is reached. That means, if we consider the factorial of 5, it is 5 multiplied by 4 multiplied by 3 multiplied by 2 multiplied by 1. Similarly, the factorial of 6 is 6 multiplied by 5 multiplied by 4 multiplied by 3 multiplied by 2 multiplied by 1.
