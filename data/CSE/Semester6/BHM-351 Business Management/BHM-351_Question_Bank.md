# BHM-351 - Business Management
## Complete Unit-Wise Question Bank (Theory + Numericals, Basic → Advanced)

> **Course:** BHM-351 - Business Management  
> **Credits:** 2  
> **Coverage:** Strictly mapped to the syllabus.

## Syllabus Reference
- UNIT-I: Meaning, need, nature and scope of business, objectives, qualities of a successful businessman, forms of business ownership, public/private/joint sector, public-private partnership, NGOs.
- UNIT-II: Meaning, emergence of management thought, characteristics of management, bureaucracy, scientific management, administrative theories, principles of management, social responsibility and business ethics.
- UNIT-III: Planning: meaning, characteristics, process, types of plans, MBO & MBE; decision making types and steps; communication - importance and types.
- UNIT-IV: Organization: meaning, characteristics, importance, steps, structure, departmentation; span of management, centralization vs decentralization; staffing - meaning, functions, selection procedure and instruments.

## UNIT I — Business Fundamentals

### Section C: Advanced Theory & Numericals
