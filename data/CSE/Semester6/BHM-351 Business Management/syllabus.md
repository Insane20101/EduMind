# BHM-351 - Business Management\n\n**Credits: 2**\n\n## UNIT-I
Meaning, need, nature and scope of business, objectives, qualities of a successful businessman, forms of
business ownership, public/private/joint sector, public-private partnership, NGOs.
## UNIT-II
Meaning, emergence of management thought, characteristics of management, bureaucracy, scientific
management, administrative theories, principles of management, social responsibility and business ethics.
## UNIT-III
Planning: meaning, characteristics, process, types of plans, MBO & MBE; decision making types and steps;
communication - importance and types.
## UNIT-IV
Organization: meaning, characteristics, importance, steps, structure, departmentation; span of management,
centralization vs decentralization; staffing - meaning, functions, selection procedure and instruments.
