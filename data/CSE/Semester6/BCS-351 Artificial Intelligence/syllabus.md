# BCS-351 - Artificial Intelligence\n\n**Credits: 4**\n\n## UNIT-I
Introduction to AI, Intelligent Agents, Solving Problems by Searching, Beyond Classical Search, Adversarial
Search, Constraint Satisfaction Problems.
## UNIT-II
Knowledge and Reasoning: Logical Agents, First-Order Logic, Inference in First-Order Logic, Classical Planning,
Knowledge Representation, Uncertain Knowledge and Reasoning, Probabilistic Reasoning over Time, Decision
Making.
## UNIT-III
Planning and Acting in Real-World: classical planning algorithms, planning graphs, Boolean satisfiability,
nondeterministic domains; genetic algorithms; probabilistic reasoning, reinforcement learning, Hidden Markov
Models.
## UNIT-IV
Forms of Learning: supervised learning, decision trees, evaluating hypotheses, fuzzy logic; logical formulation of
learning, statistical learning, Naive Bayes, EM algorithm; Natural Language Processing, perception, robotics.
