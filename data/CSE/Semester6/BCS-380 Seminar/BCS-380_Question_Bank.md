## Syllabus Reference
The course focuses on the presentation of a technical seminar on a current topic in Computer Science and Engineering, involving literature review, report preparation, and oral presentation.

## UNIT I — Technical Seminar Preparation
### Section A: Definitions & Concept Questions (Basic, 2–3 Marks)

1. **[Unit I | Topic: Seminar Objectives | Type: Theory | Difficulty: Basic]**  
   What are the primary objectives of conducting a technical seminar in Computer Science and Engineering?

2. **[Unit I | Topic: Literature Review | Type: Theory | Difficulty: Basic]**  
   Define 'literature review' and explain its importance in preparing for a seminar.

### Section B: Computational & Applied Problems (Intermediate, 7 Marks)

1. **[Unit I | Topic: Research Methodology | Type: Theory | Difficulty: Intermediate]**  
   Discuss the steps involved in conducting a literature review for a seminar topic. Provide a structured approach.

2. **[Unit I | Topic: Presentation Skills | Type: Practical | Difficulty: Intermediate]**  
   Create an outline for a seminar presentation on a chosen current topic in Computer Science, including key points to cover in each section.

### Section C: Advanced Theory & Numericals

1. **[Unit I | Topic: Technical Writing | Type: Theory | Difficulty: Advanced]**  
   Analyze the key components of a technical report and their significance in the context of a seminar presentation.

2. **[Unit I | Topic: Audience Engagement | Type: Theory | Difficulty: Advanced]**  
   Evaluate various techniques to engage an audience during a technical presentation and their impact on information retention.
