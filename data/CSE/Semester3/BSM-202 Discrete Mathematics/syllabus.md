# BSM-202 - Discrete Mathematics\n\n**Credits: 4**\n\n## UNIT-I
Set Theory, Relation and Function: operations on sets, relations and functions, binary relations, partial ordering,
equivalence relations, mathematical induction, finite/infinite/countable/uncountable sets, Cantor's diagonal
argument, power set theorem, Schroder-Bernstein theorem.
## UNIT-II
Propositional logic: syntax, semantics, valid/satisfiable/unsatisfiable formulas, examining validity of logical
arguments. Proof techniques: forward proof, proof by contradiction, contrapositive proofs, necessity and
sufficiency.
## UNIT-III
Algebraic Structures: semigroups, monoids and groups, congruence relations and quotient structures, free/cyclic
monoids and groups, permutation groups, substructures, normal subgroups, rings, integral domains, fields,
Boolean algebra and Boolean ring.
## UNIT-IV
Combinatorics: inclusion-exclusion, pigeon-hole principle, permutation, combination, summations, recurrence
relations and generating functions.
