# BHM-202 - Cyber Ethics and IPR
## Complete Unit-Wise Question Bank (Theory + Numericals, Basic → Advanced)

> **Course:** BHM-202 - Cyber Ethics and IPR  
> **Credits:** 2  
> **Coverage:** Strictly mapped to the syllabus.

## Syllabus Reference
- UNIT-I: Information Technology Act 2000: aims, objects, overview, jurisdiction, electronic governance, electronic evidence. Introduction to Cyber Laws: national/international perspective, legal issues in India, cybercrime definition, information security, mobile/wireless device crimes.
- UNIT-II: Cyber Ethics, significance and need for cyber regulations and ethics, ethics in information society, Artificial Intelligence ethics: issues and core principles, introduction to Blockchain ethics.
- UNIT-III: Intellectual Property Rights: definition, objectives and scope, cyber law and IPRs. Copyright: definition, objectives, scope, benefits, main provisions of Copyright Act, issues in cyberspace.
- UNIT-IV: Indian Patent Act: definition, objectives, types, Copyrights vs Patent. Trademarks: introduction, benefits for business, trademarks on internet, domain name registration.
